About

Leadership rooted in service

Senator David Jimkuta is a Nigerian politician serving as the senator representing the Taraba South Senatorial District. He was elected to the Nigerian Senate in 2023 on the platform of the All Progressives Congress (APC).

Since assuming office, he has remained focused on representation, public accountability, and building trust with the people of Taraba South. His political journey reflects perseverance, determination, and a strong commitment to democratic engagement.

Political Journey

From contest to mandate

01

APC Nomination

Jimkuta secured the APC nomination for the Taraba South senatorial race, setting the stage for a major political contest.

02

Legal challenge

After the party’s decision was challenged, the matter moved through the courts, where the decisions were reviewed and the legal process eventually affirmed his candidacy.

03

Election victory

In the 2023 Senate election, he won decisively against the then incumbent governor of Taraba State, Darius Ishaku, in a victory that drew national attention.
