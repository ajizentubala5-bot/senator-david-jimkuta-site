About

Leadership rooted in service

Senator David Jimkuta is a Nigerian politician serving as the senator representing the Taraba South Senatorial District. He was elected to the Nigerian Senate in 2023 on the platform of the All Progressives Congress (APC).

Since assuming office, he has remained focused on representation, public accountability, and building trust with the people of Taraba South. His political journey reflects perseverance, determination, and a strong commitment to democratic engagement.

Political Journey

From contest to mandate

01

APC Nomination

Jimkuta secured the APC nomination for the Taraba South senatorial race, setting the stage for a major political contest.

02

Legal challenge

After the party’s decision was challenged, the matter moved through the courts, where the decisions were reviewed and the legal process eventually affirmed his candidacy.

03

Election victory

In the 2023 Senate election, he won decisively against the then incumbent governor of Taraba State, Darius Ishaku, in a victory that drew national attention.

3 Years of Impactful Representation

Empowering Southern Taraba through purposeful leadership

In just three years, Senator David S.U. Jimkuta has delivered a clear message of inclusive governance and practical leadership, bringing visible progress in the areas of economic empowerment, education, infrastructure, and community welfare across Southern Taraba.

Economic Empowerment

In a bid to promote economic self-reliance and reduce poverty across Southern Taraba, Senator Jimkuta rolled out one of the most far-reaching empowerment programmes in the region’s history.

  • 80 cars
  • 100 motorcycles
  • 100 tricycles
  • 150 Keke Napep
  • 100 grinding machines
  • 100 deep freezers
  • 200 sewing machines
  • 50 car-washing machines
  • Modern agricultural tools distributed across communities

He also provided fuel support worth millions of naira to motorists after the subsidy removal and distributed essential food items to all 52 wards in the district, affirming his compassion and commitment to community welfare.

Education and Human Capital Development

Recognising education as the foundation for sustainable development, Senator Jimkuta invested heavily in supporting students and educational institutions across Southern Taraba.

  • Sponsorship of JAMB registration fees for over 2,000 students
  • Donation of millions of naira to offset tuition fees for final-year students at Kwararafa University, Wukari
  • Scholarship for 40 students from Southern Taraba to study in India
  • Renovation and upgrade of the Wukari State Library into a modern E-library

These efforts are designed to nurture the next generation of leaders, professionals, and change-makers for Taraba State and Nigeria.

Infrastructure and Community Development

Senator Jimkuta also prioritised infrastructure as a catalyst for economic growth and social progress.

  • Electrification and connection of Kyaor, Jandikula, Riti, Ando Igba, Sondi, Vaase, and Kente communities to the national grid
  • Facilitation of critical road and community development projects across the district
  • Support for medical bills at the General Hospital, Wukari

These interventions reflect a lasting commitment to improving access, healthcare, connectivity, and the quality of life of the people.

Through these bold and people-oriented initiatives, Senator David S.U. Jimkuta has continued to redefine leadership in Southern Taraba, combining compassion, accountability, and measurable results to build a stronger future for the people.
